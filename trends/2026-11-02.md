## 1. AI Automation Agencies

### What's NEW
- **Value-based pricing accelerating:** Agencies are increasingly quoting 10–20% of measurable annual client savings rather than flat project fees — this model is landing in mid-market deals where ROI is easy to calculate (e.g., FTE hours recaptured, calls handled without staff).
- **Small-business starter packages gaining market share:** $2,500–$7,500 one-time build + $300–$800/month maintenance is emerging as the SMB entry point to compete against no-code tools.
- **VapiCon 2026 (Nov 11–12, San Francisco):** Vapi's first public conference — signals the voice AI agency ecosystem is maturing into a professional category. Expect platform roadmap drops and new pricing announcements.
- **"In-house AI operator" model spreading:** Flat $3,600/month + $3,000 placement for a dedicated AI operations role — agencies packaging this as a staff-augmentation alternative to traditional retainers.

### What's WORKING
- **Four-tier pricing ladder (proven):** Starter ($500–$800/mo) → Growth ($1K–$2.5K/mo) → Business ($3K–$7K/mo) → Enterprise (custom). Each tier derisks the upsell path.
- **Project fee ranges by complexity:** single-workflow builds $1,500–$12,000; full automation stacks $5,000–$75,000; hourly consulting $100–$450/hour.
- **Paid audits as pipeline:** $1,500–$2,000 AI Readiness Audit reduces prospect commitment risk and converts at high rates to retainer.
- **Outcome-based addendum:** Add a performance kicker (% of savings) on top of retainer once baseline metrics are set — clients see it as shared upside, agencies earn more from their best work.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Launch a published price page.** The market now expects it — comparison buyers are eliminating agencies without transparent pricing before they ever reply to outreach.
- **Package value-based pricing for RCM and clinic scheduling clients** where cost-per-call and appointments-booked are auditable — the 10–20% savings model can yield $2K–$8K/month from a single clinic.
- **Position at VapiCon even without attending:** publish a "what we expect from VapiCon" post pre-conference, then a "what it means for our clients" recap post-conference. Earns inbound from voice AI builders searching after the event.

## 5. AI for Verticals

### What's NEW

#### Healthcare
- **State AI legislation accelerating (2026 passed):** Alabama, Georgia, Iowa, Utah, and Washington all passed laws restricting AI as the sole basis for coverage denials in health insurance. Indiana (effective July 1, 2026) bars AI from downcoding claims without a licensed healthcare professional's review.
- **Washington state** now requires a licensed physician or health professional to deny requests on medical necessity grounds — AI cannot be the sole mechanism.
- **Class actions against UnitedHealth, Humana, Cigna:** Lawsuits allege algorithmic tools improperly denied Medicare Advantage coverage. Contract-based challenges to AI-driven coverage decisions are opening a new litigation front.
- **NAIC Model Bulletin** on insurer AI use is the active regulatory reference for state insurance commissioners — compliance-aware agencies should read and reference it.

#### Legal
- **Legal AI moving to integrated production systems:** Law firms shifting from isolated AI tools (standalone research assistants) toward full workflow integration — intake → conflict check → drafting → billing. This is an agency build opportunity.
- **Confidentiality + privilege remain the key objections** — agencies that address these first (data-isolation architecture, on-prem or private cloud options) win legal deals faster.

#### Real Estate
- **Real estate + home services remain the lowest-barrier AI agency niche.** Speed-to-lead automation, lead follow-up sequences, and appointment scheduling are confirmed high-ROI use cases. Retainer range: $2,000–$4,000/month.
- **Note: real estate is crowded.** Multiple sources flag it as saturated. Differentiate by specializing in a sub-vertical (commercial, luxury, property management) or bundling MLS data integration.

#### Insurance
- **Insurance compliance AI is a new category.** NAIC bulletin compliance, AI audit trails for coverage decisions, and human-in-the-loop documentation are emerging as paid services post-state-law wave.
- **FNOL (First Notice of Loss) agentic automation** — claims intake via voice agent + auto-triage workflow — is the highest-ROI automation for P&C insurers.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Build a "NAIC Bulletin Compliance Package" for insurance clients** — AI audit trail + human-review routing + documentation. The regulatory environment is creating demand that didn't exist 6 months ago. Price at $3,500–$8,000 build + $1,500–$3,000/mo retainer.
- **Target legal intake first, not full practice management.** Intake screening, conflict-check prep, and matter-opening workflows are where legal AI is being bought — they're also low-risk builds (no legal advice involved).
- **In healthcare, lead with voice agent scheduling** (still the highest ROI per dollar, lowest compliance complexity) rather than clinical or billing AI — faster sales cycle, easier implementation.
- **For real estate, go sub-vertical:** property management companies (tenant intake, maintenance routing, lease renewal) are underserved and less competitive than residential sales.

## 6. Content & Positioning

### What's NEW
- **"AI scales clarity, it can't create it" is the emerging agency counter-narrative.** After two years of generic "AI-powered" messaging flooding every channel, top agencies are now differentiating by leading with brand POV and human judgment — AI is the production layer, not the headline.
- **AEO/GEO as a standard agency service:** Answer Engine Optimization (getting clients cited when buyers ask AI tools for vendor recommendations) is now in most mid-tier agency service menus. Buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude for shortlists before ever Googling.
- **"AI-free" positioning is contested:** Some agencies are using "human-only" as differentiation and reporting success. The data is inconclusive — human judgment + AI tools seems to outperform both pure-AI and pure-human production on conversion metrics.
- **Forbes Agency Council (Feb 2026) warning still relevant:** AI-generated messaging creates the most predictable, undifferentiated creative in the category. Agencies that train AI on their own language and client realities instead of generic prompts are pulling ahead.

### What's WORKING
- **Brand POV before AI terminology:** Messaging that leads with client outcomes (time saved, revenue gained, compliance achieved) converts better than "AI-powered" claims without evidence.
- **Case study format:** Specific, verifiable before/after metrics (X calls handled per day, Y% reduction in no-shows, Z hours of admin eliminated) are the highest-converting content format for AI agencies in 2026.
- **LinkedIn depth over breadth:** Long-form posts (800–1,200 words) with specific methodology and client-voice quotes continue to outperform short posts. Carousels with annotated workflow screenshots drive comments and saves.
- **AEO content:** Write content structured to answer "what is the best AI automation agency for [vertical]?" queries — structured FAQs, comparison tables, and specific service descriptions indexed by AI crawlers.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Publish one detailed case study per month** with verifiable metrics from a real client. This is the single highest-ROI content investment for an AI agency — it feeds both human buyers and AI recommendation engines.
- **Lead with compliance and ROI, not technology.** For healthcare, insurance, and legal verticals — the three currently hottest — buyers care about risk reduction and measurable return, not which LLM is under the hood.
- **Claim AEO territory now:** Write structured service pages targeting "best voice AI agency for [clinic/insurance/real estate]" queries — these are being answered by ChatGPT and Perplexity from indexed web content. Own the answer before a competitor does.
- **Human + AI production framing:** Position TRIVOX as "AI-accelerated human expertise" — keeps the human accountability story intact while acknowledging AI as the efficiency layer. Test this against "AI-first" messaging in your next 10 outreach sequences.
