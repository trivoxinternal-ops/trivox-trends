## 1. AI Automation Agencies

### 🆕 What's NEW (Last 7 Days)

- **Multi-agent orchestration is the new standard service tier:** The conversation has shifted from "we build workflows" to "we deploy multi-agent systems." Agencies winning large deals in Q3 2026 are pitching architectures where a supervisor agent routes tasks to specialized sub-agents (intake, scheduling, CRM update, follow-up). The sell is an autonomous operations layer, not individual automations.
- **Agency retainer compression at the low end:** Monthly retainers under $1,500/mo are disappearing from winning agency pricing pages. The justification: AI maintenance, monitoring, and iteration require genuine engineering hours; sub-$1,500 retainers attract clients who expect too much for too little. Agencies that have raised their floor report faster closes and lower churn.
- **AI agency white-label demand surging:** Traditional marketing agencies and SaaS consultancies are approaching AI automation shops for white-label partnerships — they have the clients, lack the technical depth. This is a new revenue stream: charge $2K–$5K/mo per white-label partner for infrastructure access and co-delivery.
- **The PlayHT migration window has closed:** Agencies that hadn't migrated PlayHT-dependent client builds by this week are encountering active API failures. Any remaining client disruptions are now reputation incidents, not just technical debt.
- **Compliance-as-a-service becoming its own category:** Post-EU AI Act, Arizona HB 2175, and state-level AI bills, compliance documentation and audit trails are being sold as standalone line items. Agencies billing separately for compliance deliverables (model cards, output logging, human-review checkpoints) report 15–25% higher total contract values.

### ✅ What's WORKING

- **Managed operations framing** ("we run your AI systems, not just build them") generates significantly stickier retainers — clients stay 3× longer vs. build-and-handoff engagements
- **White-label partnerships with non-AI agencies** — zero marketing spend, warm intros, and clients who are already sold on the concept
- **Compliance line items** — separating compliance deliverables from build deliverables creates a new billable surface and positions the agency as a long-term risk management partner, not just a tech vendor

### 🎯 TRIVOX Should Consider

- Package a white-label partner program — define terms, pricing, and a delivery playbook; approach 3–5 marketing or CRM agencies this week with a one-page proposal
- Add a compliance deliverable (audit trail documentation + human-review checkpoint design) as a standard line item on all new healthcare, insurance, and legal builds — price at $1,500–$3,000/build
- Update all pricing pages to reflect a $2,000/mo minimum retainer floor; deprecate any sub-$2K tier language
