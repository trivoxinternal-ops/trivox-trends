## 1. AI Automation Agencies

### 🆕 What's NEW (Last 7 Days)

- **Outcome-based pricing is now the majority model:** Industry surveys this week confirm that most new AI automation agency contracts in 2026 are structured around results — booked appointments, leads generated, hours saved — rather than hourly or pure retainer fees. The phrase "we charge for what the automation delivers" is now standard sales language, not a differentiator.
- **Six dominant pricing models codified:** Discovery audit ($0–$5K) → single workflow build ($5K–$25K) → multi-workflow system ($25K–$100K+) → monthly retainer ($2K–$15K/mo) → performance/revenue-share → hourly consulting ($150–$400/hr). Agencies winning the most deals in September are explicitly offering a tiered menu of these, not a single price point.
- **Agentic behavior is now a baseline client expectation:** Clients in 2026 arrive expecting AI summarization, routing, decision-support, and lightweight agent-like behavior across their existing tools. "We build workflows" is no longer sufficient positioning — "we deploy agents that operate your business processes" is the frame that generates inbound.
- **LinkedIn Ads emerge as top paid channel for AI agencies:** Multiple agency founders report LinkedIn outperforming Google Ads for B2B AI services in September, particularly for targeting operations directors, practice managers, and clinic owners. Cost-per-qualified-lead is higher but conversion-to-signed-client rates are 3–4× better.
- **PlayHT acquired by Meta and discontinued as a standalone platform** — this removes a popular TTS option from many agency stacks. Agencies should audit any client builds using PlayHT APIs immediately.

### ✅ What's WORKING

- **Discovery audit as the entry point** ($1,500–$3,000 paid) continues to be the highest-converting sales motion — it disqualifies bad-fit clients, creates a documented ROI case, and pre-sells the build
- **Single-vertical, single-pain-point positioning** closes faster than general AI agency messaging; agencies that own one niche (e.g., "AI voice for dental clinics") are commanding 20–30% price premiums
- **Case study-driven content on LinkedIn** is the top organic acquisition channel; specific metrics (e.g., "this clinic recaptured 38 appointments/month") outperform all other content formats
