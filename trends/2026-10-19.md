## 1. AI Automation Agencies

### What's NEW
- **Outcome-based pricing accelerating:** More agencies are moving to per-workflow flat fees (e.g., $5K per workflow that saves 10+ hours/week) rather than hourly or pure retainer models — signaling a shift toward ROI-tied pricing.
- **Audit-first funnels standard:** The $750–$1,500 readiness audit credited toward a build is now industry-standard as the low-friction entry point.
- **Market saturation intensifying:** The top search results for "AI automation agency 2026" now return 10+ agency ranking lists — the space is commoditizing fast, making vertical depth and proof-of-results non-negotiable for differentiation.

### What's WORKING
- **Tiered pricing stack:**
  - Starter workflow: $1,000–$3,500 (1–2 automations)
  - Core build: $5,000–$15,000 (multi-system)
  - Deep specialization: $10,000–$20,000+ setup + $2,000–$5,000/month retainer
  - Agent License model: $20K setup + $2K/month ongoing (premium positioning)
- **Specialization over breadth:** Agencies narrowly focused on one vertical (clinics, real estate, legal) are winning against generalists at every price tier.
- **Proof-first sales:** Agencies leading with case study ROI numbers (e.g., 675% ROI in 90 days) before pitching services are converting at higher rates.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Introduce an explicit "Agent License" tier** at $20K setup + $2K/month — clients see it as infrastructure, not a service, reducing churn.
- **Make the audit the default first step** — not a free consultation, a paid deliverable ($750–$1,500) that reduces your time-to-close and filters serious buyers.
- **Keep all messaging outcome-specific:** "Saves 12 hours/week on intake" beats "AI-powered automation" — the latter is now noise.

## 5. AI for Verticals

### Healthcare
- **Pennsylvania HB 1925:** House-approved; moves to Senate committees. Restricts AI as sole basis for coverage decisions — same wave as Iowa HF 2635 (effective Oct 1). Human-review requirements are becoming law across states.
- **NAIC AI Risk Evaluation Supplement:** Public comment period closes October 2026, final adoption targeted November 2026 NAIC Fall Meeting. Insurers and health plans will face standardized AI disclosure requirements.
- **Litigation:** Cigna lost a California federal case over its automated medical-necessity review process. Class actions against algorithmic denial are accelerating — malpractice risk for uncovered AI decisions is now real.
- **TRIVOX angle:** Healthcare voice AI for scheduling and triage is still the safest revenue stream. Avoid any positioning around clinical decision support — it's a regulatory minefield. Retell (HIPAA-default) is the right stack.

### Real Estate
- **DTRE AI Data Platform (Oct 15):** Commercial real estate firm launched proprietary AI for deal sourcing and market intelligence — signals enterprise adoption accelerating.
- **Moveli AI for Brokers (Oct ~15):** AI tool enabling brokers to create personalized property proposals post-valuation — exactly the kind of SMB tool TRIVOX can automate around.
- **EliseAI Apollo:** Agentic AI teammate for property operations — handles any task on the Elise platform autonomously. Strongest multifamily product yet.
- **Entrata Forge:** LLM built specifically for multifamily real estate, rolling out to customers now. Watch for workflow automation use cases.
- **Atlia (YC-backed):** Autonomous AI agents managing short-term rentals end-to-end — cutting management fees in half. Proof point that full-autonomy property operations are viable.
- **TRIVOX angle:** Broker lead qualification + follow-up + proposal generation is a high-ROI, low-regulatory-risk package. Bundle voice outreach (Bland/Retell) + CRM update (HubSpot/GHL) + proposal draft (Claude).

### Insurance
- **AI insurance market at $8.63B (2025 base):** Still growing rapidly, driven by claims automation and underwriting AI.
- **Human-in-the-loop mandates spreading:** Texas, Arizona, and proposed Puerto Rico bulletin all requiring human approval for consequential AI decisions. This is a selling point, not an obstacle — position TRIVOX's workflows as "human-in-the-loop by design."
- **FNOL automation (agentic):** First Notice of Loss automation is the breakout insurance workflow — captures incident data, initiates claim, routes to adjuster with a summary. Strong ROI, clear scope.
- **TRIVOX angle:** FNOL automation + outbound renewal calls + compliance-ready workflow design is a differentiated insurance package. Emphasize HITL architecture in proposals.

### Legal
- **Harvey:** Still the enterprise standard ($1,200–$2,000+/user/month). Increasingly used for contract review, due diligence, and litigation research.
- **Thomson Reuters CoCounsel Legal:** Best for firms standardized on TR research stack.
- **Spellbook:** Leading tool for in-Word contract drafting and review.
- **Lawmatics:** Strongest for legal client intake, lead scoring, and CRM automation — closest to TRIVOX's wheelhouse.
- **TRIVOX angle:** Legal intake automation (Lawmatics + voice agent for lead qualification) is the easiest legal vertical entry. Avoid contract AI (crowded, requires legal expertise). Focus on intake → CRM → follow-up as a standalone product.

## 6. Content & Positioning

### What's NEW
- **AEO/GEO is the new SEO:** AI search tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) are generating shortlists for buyers — agencies that aren't optimized for AI-citation are losing top-of-funnel. Google AI Overviews are cutting organic traffic 18–47% for some categories.
- **"Human perspective" positioning outperforms "AI-powered":** The strongest-performing agency positioning in 2026 is not "we use AI" — it's a distinct, human-owned point of view that AI helps execute at scale. "AI scales clarity but can't create it" is the new copywriting principle.
- **Specialization + proof = conversion:** Third-party case studies, measurable ROI numbers, and vertical depth are the three factors buyers cite most in agency selection. Generic AI claims are tuned out.

### What's WORKING
- **Demo-first outbound:** Leading with a working demo (voice agent calling your prospect's own number, or a live workflow demo) converts ~36% better than deck-first approaches.
- **LinkedIn carousels + stat posts:** Carousels showing before/after workflows with specific time/cost savings get 6.6%+ engagement. Posts with hard numbers ("saved 12 hours/week") outperform conceptual AI posts.
- **YouTube niche content:** Tutorial-style videos ("Build a clinic scheduling bot in n8n") pull warm leads — viewers already pre-sold on automation before they reach out.
- **Vertical-specific case studies:** A single strong case study in your target vertical (e.g., "675% ROI for a clinic in 90 days") outperforms five generic testimonials.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Audit your Google/AI search presence:** Search "AI automation agency for clinics" in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude. If TRIVOX isn't cited, invest in content that earns citations (original data, named case studies, how-to guides with measurable outcomes).
- **Lead every LinkedIn post with a number:** Time saved, revenue recovered, cost per call — specific beats vague every time.
- **Build one "hero demo"** per vertical (clinic, real estate, insurance) — a short recorded demo showing a real problem being solved in real-time. Use it as the first message in cold outreach.
- **Replace "AI-powered" in all copy with outcome language:** "Answers 200 patient calls per day without staff" > "AI-powered phone answering."
