## 1. AI Automation Agencies

### What's NEW
- **Agent licensing model gaining traction:** A $20K setup fee + $2K/month "Agent License" structure is emerging as a premium tier above standard retainers — appealing to clients who want dedicated, always-on automation.
- **Hybrid pricing is now the SMB default:** Fixed-fee build + small monthly retainer has replaced the pure hourly model for most SMB engagements.
- **Hundreds of overlapping agencies:** The AI automation agency space is now extremely crowded, with agencies competing on identical "AI-native / AI-powered / AI-first" claims — differentiation is the #1 growth problem.

### What's WORKING
- **Project fees:** $1,000–$3,500 for starter workflow (1–2 core automations); $8,000–$45,000 for complex, multi-system builds.
- **Retainers:** $500–$6,000/month for ongoing optimization; top agencies charging $3,000–$5,000/month for vertical-specialized maintenance.
- **Hourly consulting:** $100–$450/hour — premium for AI strategy and architecture work.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Adopt agent licensing language** — "Agent License" is a differentiating pricing mechanism that signals ongoing value vs. one-time build.
- **Pick 1–2 verticals and own them** — generalist messaging is being drowned out; clinics + real estate is TRIVOX's sharpest edge.
- **Bundle audit + build:** Offer a $500–$1,500 "AI Readiness Audit" as a foot-in-the-door that converts to $5K+ builds.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
