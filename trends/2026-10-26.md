## 1. AI Automation Agencies

### What's NEW
- **Salesforce Hunter launched Oct 16** — an AI outbound sales agent on Agentforce, powered by ZoomInfo and Demandbase for prospect/account intelligence. Priced at $2/conversation (Agentforce platform rate). Direct competition to agency-built outbound agents.
- **AgentSpot multiplayer AI platform (Oct 22)** — transitions customer ops from single-agent chatbots to collaborative multi-agent teams sharing context. Signals that multi-agent orchestration is now a mainstream buyer expectation, not a premium upsell.
- **Demandbase AI agents (Oct 22)** — identify account intent signals, draft multi-channel outreach, and auto-update CRM records. B2B agencies need to price against this automation.

### What's WORKING
- **Hybrid pricing is the 2026 default for SMBs:** one-time build fee + monthly retainer. Typical ranges: $5K–$50K build, $2K–$15K/month retainer, $100–$300/hour.
- **Paid audits as the entry point:** workflow readiness audits priced $1,500–$2,000 convert prospects by reducing commitment risk before a full engagement.
- **Tiered retainers:** Essential ($500–$1K/month) → Growth ($1K–$2.5K/month) → Custom. This ladder allows upsell without re-selling.
- **In-house operator model** gaining traction: flat $3,600/month + $3,000 one-time placement fee for a dedicated AI operator — appeals to mid-market companies wanting staff-like continuity.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- Package a "Hunter Competitor" outbound voice agent stack (VAPI + CRM sync + ZoomInfo data) as a direct alternative to Salesforce Hunter for companies not on Agentforce.
- Add a $1,500 "AI Readiness Audit" as a standard entry offer to fill the pipeline — low friction, high conversion to retainer.
- Build the multi-agent customer ops pitch: one inbound agent, one outbound agent, one CRM-sync agent working as a coordinated squad.

## 6. Content & Positioning

### What's NEW
- **Answer-engine visibility (AEO) is the primary content target in 2026.** Buyers ask AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) for vendor recommendations. The goal is to be cited inside AI answers. Full "Generative Visibility" takes 6–9 months of technical optimization — agencies should sell this as a service.
- **Clear POV beats volume:** Heinz Marketing's Oct 2026 research confirms that producing more AI content doesn't work — a human-owned point of view trained on your own positioning is the differentiator.
- **AI-free positioning is risky.** A documented B2B SaaS case shows a 34% organic traffic decline after avoiding AI optimization. Agencies pitching "we don't use AI" are losing.
- **Credibility signals matter for AEO:** Author attribution, H2 structure, bullet takeaways, concise TL;DR — these are the structural signals AI tools use to decide whether to cite a page.
- **Content retainers:** $2,500–$10,000/month for agencies offering AEO-optimized content strategy.

### What's WORKING
- **"Human perspective" framing** continues to outperform "AI-powered" in agency messaging (confirmed across prior weeks + reinforced this week).
- **LinkedIn carousels at 6.6% engagement** (from prior weeks) still the top format.
- **Short, structured content:** TL;DR + bullets + clear H2s. The algorithmic and AI-citation stack both reward scannability over depth.
- **Demo-first outbound:** showing a live agent demo in the first touch (rather than a deck) continues to drive higher meeting conversion.

### TRIVOX Should Consider
- **Sell AEO as a service:** "Get TRIVOX cited when someone asks ChatGPT for a voice agent vendor." This is new, has no commodity competition yet, and directly ties to lead generation.
- **Audit TRIVOX's own content for AEO signals:** Add author bios, structured H2s, bullet TL;DRs to every service page. This is a 2-hour fix with compounding return.
- **Position TRIVOX as an "AI operations partner," not an "AI agency"** (the +18–24% open rate framing confirmed in earlier weeks remains valid).
- **Post a POV piece on n8n 3.0 migration risks:** timely, useful to technical buyers, positions TRIVOX as the authority before competitors notice.
